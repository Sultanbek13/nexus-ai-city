# NEXUS AI City

An interactive 3D AI-powered smart city, built for the Faculty of Business & Technology "Build Your Own Smart City" assignment.

**Live demo:** https://sultanbek13.github.io/nexus-ai-city/

## Features
- Full 3D city with 360° rotate, zoom and pan controls
- 13 clickable districts (FinTech bank, startup hub, e-commerce mall, AI command center, robot logistics, smart grid, data center, hospital, university, factory, mobility hub, park, smart homes)
- Enter any building to explore a 3D interior with clickable objects
- Information panels with live KPIs, charts and AI insights
- AI vs. fixed-timer traffic lights, day/night mode, guided tour, "Ask NEXUS AI" assistant

Built with Three.js. Author: Sultanbek.
